Chicken Francese
Chicken Francese
Serves 4
Ingredients
  • 2 large chicken breasts, halved lengthwise
  • ½ cup all-purpose flour
  • 3 eggs, beaten
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter, divided
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • ½ cup dry white wine
  • ¾ cup chicken stock
  • 3 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp capers, drained
  • 2 tbsp flat-leaf parsley, chopped
Instructions
  1. Season cutlets well. Dredge in flour, shaking off excess, then coat in beaten egg.
  2. Heat oil and 1 tbsp butter over medium-high until shimmering. Cook cutlets 3 min per side until deep golden. Remove and tent with foil.
  3. Reduce heat to medium. Add garlic and cook 30 seconds. Pour in wine and reduce by half, scraping up the fond.
  4. Add stock and lemon juice. Simmer 3 min until slightly thickened.
  5. Off heat, swirl in remaining cold butter to finish the sauce. Stir in capers.
  6. Return chicken, spoon sauce over, and garnish with parsley.
Tips
  • Cold butter off heat is the key to a silky, emulsified sauce.
  • Don't skip the fond — those browned bits are where the flavor lives.

Shrimp Risotto
Shrimp Risotto
Serves 4
Ingredients
  • 1½ lb large shrimp, peeled & deveined
  • Chili flakes, to taste
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter, divided
  • 1 shallot, finely diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1½ cups Arborio rice
  • ½ cup dry white wine
  • 5 cups warm seafood or chicken stock
  • ½ cup Parmesan, grated
  • 1 lemon (zest & juice)
  • 2 tbsp flat-leaf parsley, chopped
Instructions
  1. Season shrimp. Sear in 1 tbsp butter and oil over high heat, 1–2 min per side until pink. Set aside.
  2. Reduce heat to medium. Add remaining butter and shallot, cook 3 min until soft. Add garlic, cook 1 min.
  3. Add rice and toast 2 min, stirring constantly, until edges turn translucent.
  4. Pour in wine and stir until fully absorbed.
  5. Add warm stock one ladle at a time, stirring frequently and waiting until each addition is absorbed, about 18–20 min total.
  6. Stir in Parmesan, lemon zest and juice. Fold shrimp back in, rest 1 min off heat, and serve topped with parsley.
Tips
  • Keep stock warm — cold stock slows cooking and affects texture.
  • Sear shrimp separately so they don't overcook in the risotto.

Beef Stroganoff
Beef Stroganoff
Serves 4
Ingredients
  • 1½ lb beef sirloin, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter, divided
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced
  • 8 oz cremini mushrooms, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup beef stock
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • ¾ cup sour cream
Instructions
  1. Season beef well. Sear in 1 tbsp butter and oil over high heat in batches, 1 min per side. Set aside — don't crowd the pan.
  2. Reduce to medium. Add remaining butter and onion, cook 5 min until softened.
  3. Add mushrooms and cook 4–5 min until golden. Add garlic and cook 1 min.
  4. Sprinkle flour over and stir 1 min. Add stock and Worcestershire, scraping up the fond. Simmer 3–4 min until thickened.
  5. Stir in Dijon and sour cream. Warm through gently — do not boil.
  6. Return beef and any juices. Stir gently, season to taste, and serve over egg noodles with fresh parsley.
Tips
  • Sear in batches over high heat so the beef browns rather than steams.
  • Never boil after adding sour cream or it will curdle.

Baked Potato Soup
Baked Potato Soup
Serves 4
Ingredients
  • 4 large russet potatoes, peeled & cubed
  • 4 strips bacon, chopped
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 3 cups chicken stock
  • 2 cups whole milk
  • ½ cup sour cream
  • 1 cup cheddar cheese, shredded
  • 3 green onions, sliced
Instructions
  1. Cook bacon in a large pot over medium heat until crispy. Remove and set aside, leaving the fat in the pot.
  2. Add butter and onion. Cook 4–5 min until softened. Add garlic and cook 1 min.
  3. Sprinkle flour over and stir 1 min. Pour in stock gradually, whisking until smooth.
  4. Add potatoes and bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer 15–18 min until tender.
  5. Lightly mash some potatoes in the pot to thicken. Stir in milk and sour cream. Warm through — do not boil.
  6. Season to taste and serve topped with cheddar, bacon, and green onions.
Tips
  • Russet potatoes break down better than waxy varieties — ideal for a creamy texture.
  • Don't boil after adding dairy or the soup may curdle.

Crab Cakes
Crab Cakes
Serves 4
Ingredients
  • 1 lb lump crab meat, picked over
  • ¼ cup mayonnaise
  • 1 large egg, beaten
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp Old Bay seasoning
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp flat-leaf parsley, chopped
  • ½ cup panko breadcrumbs
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
Instructions
  1. Gently combine mayo, egg, mustard, Old Bay, Worcestershire, lemon juice, and parsley in a bowl.
  2. Fold in crab meat and panko, being careful not to break up the lumps. Season to taste.
  3. Shape into 8 patties about ¾ inch thick. Refrigerate at least 30 min to firm up.
  4. Heat butter and oil in a large skillet over medium-high until the foam subsides.
  5. Cook crab cakes 3–4 min per side until deeply golden and heated through. Work in batches if needed.
  6. Serve with lemon wedges and remoulade or tartar sauce.
Tips
  • Chilling is non-negotiable — it's what keeps them from falling apart in the pan.
  • Less filler means better crab cakes; add just enough panko to bind.

Spaghetti Carbonara
Spaghetti Carbonara
Serves 4
Ingredients
  • 12 oz spaghetti
  • 200g pancetta or guanciale, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, lightly crushed
  • 4 large egg yolks
  • 1 whole egg
  • 80g Pecorino Romano, finely grated
  • 40g Parmesan, finely grated
  • 1 cup pasta water, reserved
Instructions
  1. Boil spaghetti in heavily salted water until al dente. Reserve 1 cup pasta water before draining.
  2. Cook pancetta in a large skillet over medium heat until crispy. Add garlic for 1 min, then remove garlic.
  3. Whisk egg yolks, whole egg, Pecorino, and Parmesan together. Season very generously with black pepper.
  4. Remove skillet from heat and let cool 1 min. Add drained pasta and toss to coat in the fat.
  5. Pour egg mixture over pasta, tossing quickly. Add pasta water a splash at a time until the sauce is silky and coats every strand.
  6. Serve immediately with extra cheese and a crack of black pepper.
Tips
  • Never add cream — the creaminess comes entirely from eggs, cheese, and starchy pasta water.
  • Off the heat is critical when adding the egg mixture or it will scramble.

Beef Wellington
Beef Wellington
Serves 4
Ingredients
  • 2 lb center-cut beef tenderloin
  • 1 lb cremini mushrooms, finely chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 shallots, minced
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 6 thin slices prosciutto
  • 1 sheet puff pastry, thawed
  • 2 egg yolks, beaten (egg wash)
  • 2 tbsp fresh thyme leaves
Instructions
  1. Season tenderloin generously. Sear in hot oil 2 min per side until browned all over. Brush with Dijon and let cool completely.
  2. Cook mushrooms, shallots, garlic, and thyme in the same pan over medium heat, stirring, until all moisture evaporates, about 15 min. Cool completely.
  3. Lay prosciutto in overlapping slices on plastic wrap. Spread mushroom mixture evenly over prosciutto. Place tenderloin at edge and roll tightly into a log. Refrigerate 30 min.
  4. Wrap in puff pastry, sealing seams with egg wash. Brush all over with egg wash. Score lightly. Chill 15 min.
  5. Bake at 425°F (220°C) for 25–30 min until pastry is deep golden and internal temp reads 125°F (52°C) for medium-rare.
  6. Rest 10 min before slicing.
Tips
  • The duxelles (mushroom mixture) must be completely dry or the pastry will be soggy.
  • Chilling at each stage keeps the layers firm and the shape tight.

Meatballs
Meatballs
Serves 4
Ingredients
  • 1 lb ground beef (80/20)
  • ½ lb ground pork
  • ½ cup breadcrumbs, soaked in ¼ cup milk
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ cup Parmesan, grated
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • ¼ cup flat-leaf parsley, chopped
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • ½ tsp red pepper flakes
  • 28 oz canned crushed San Marzano tomatoes
Instructions
  1. Combine beef, pork, soaked breadcrumbs, eggs, Parmesan, garlic, parsley, oregano, and pepper flakes. Mix gently until just combined — do not overwork.
  2. Roll into 1½-inch balls. Refrigerate 20 min to firm up.
  3. Brown meatballs in olive oil over medium-high heat, turning to sear all sides, about 6 min. Remove and set aside.
  4. In the same pan, sauté a little garlic in olive oil, then add crushed tomatoes. Season and simmer 10 min.
  5. Nestle meatballs into sauce. Cover and simmer on low for 20 min.
  6. Serve over pasta or with crusty bread, topped with extra Parmesan.
Tips
  • Soaking breadcrumbs in milk (panade) keeps the meatballs tender and moist.
  • Mix with your hands just until combined — overworking makes them tough.

Shrimp Scampi
Shrimp Scampi
Serves 4
Ingredients
  • 1½ lb large shrimp, peeled & deveined
  • 16 oz linguine
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 6 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • ½ tsp red pepper flakes
  • ½ cup dry white wine
  • 1 lemon, juice & zest
  • ½ cup pasta water, reserved
  • ¼ cup flat-leaf parsley, chopped
Instructions
  1. Cook linguine in salted boiling water until al dente. Reserve ½ cup pasta water before draining.
  2. Pat shrimp dry and season. Sear in 1 tbsp butter and olive oil over high heat, 1 min per side. Remove and set aside.
  3. Reduce heat to medium. Add remaining butter, garlic, and pepper flakes. Cook 1–2 min until garlic is fragrant but not browned.
  4. Pour in white wine and lemon juice. Simmer 2 min, scraping up any bits.
  5. Add pasta and a splash of pasta water. Toss to coat, adding more water as needed for a glossy sauce.
  6. Return shrimp, toss together, and finish with lemon zest and parsley.
Tips
  • Don't overcook the shrimp — pull them off heat while still slightly underdone as they'll finish in the sauce.
  • Pasta water is the secret to a silky, emulsified sauce that clings to every noodle.

Chicken Tikka Masala
Chicken Tikka Masala
Serves 4
Ingredients
  • 1½ lb boneless chicken thighs, cubed
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 2 tsp garam masala
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 14 oz canned crushed tomatoes
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 2 tbsp fresh cilantro, chopped
Instructions
  1. Combine chicken with yogurt, lemon juice, 1 tsp garam masala, and salt. Marinate at least 30 min.
  2. Cook chicken over high heat in a pan or under the broiler until charred in spots, 8–10 min. Set aside.
  3. Heat oil over medium. Cook onion 6–8 min until golden. Add garlic and ginger; cook 2 min.
  4. Stir in remaining garam masala and paprika. Cook 1 min until fragrant.
  5. Add tomatoes and simmer 10 min. Stir in cream and simmer 5 min until thickened.
  6. Add chicken and simmer 5 min to heat through. Garnish with cilantro and serve with rice or naan.
Tips
  • Marinating overnight makes a big difference — yogurt tenderizes the chicken and deepens the flavor.
  • A little char on the chicken is authentic and adds depth to the sauce.

Chocolate Lava Cake
Chocolate Lava Cake
Serves 4
Ingredients
  • 4 oz dark chocolate (70%), chopped
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 egg yolks
  • ¼ cup granulated sugar
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 1 pinch of salt
  • 1 tbsp cocoa powder, for dusting
Instructions
  1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Butter 4 ramekins and dust with cocoa powder, tapping out the excess.
  2. Melt chocolate and butter together over a double boiler or in 30-second microwave bursts, stirring until smooth. Cool slightly.
  3. Whisk eggs, yolks, and sugar until pale and slightly thickened, about 2 min.
  4. Fold chocolate mixture into eggs. Stir in flour and salt until just combined.
  5. Divide batter among ramekins. Bake 12–13 min until edges are set but the center still jiggles.
  6. Rest 1 min. Run a knife around the edge, invert onto plates, and serve immediately.
Tips
  • The center should jiggle like Jell-O when you pull it — it firms as it rests.
  • Fill ramekins up to 24 hrs ahead and refrigerate; add 1–2 min to bake time from cold.

Eggs Benedict
Eggs Benedict
Serves 4
Ingredients
  • 4 English muffins, split & toasted
  • 8 slices Canadian bacon
  • 8 large eggs
  • 2 tbsp white vinegar
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • ½ cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 pinch cayenne pepper
Instructions
  1. Hollandaise: whisk egg yolks and lemon juice in a heatproof bowl over barely simmering water until thickened, 3–4 min. Slowly drizzle in butter, whisking constantly. Season with cayenne and salt. Keep warm over the hot water off heat.
  2. Warm Canadian bacon in a pan over medium, 1 min per side.
  3. Poach eggs: bring a wide pan of water to a gentle simmer and add vinegar. Crack eggs in and poach 3–4 min until whites are set but yolks are runny. Remove with a slotted spoon.
  4. Assemble: toasted muffin half, Canadian bacon, poached egg, generous spoon of hollandaise.
Tips
  • Fresh eggs poach best — older eggs spread in the water and lose their shape.
  • Make hollandaise last and keep it warm while you poach — it doesn't reheat well.

Pillowy Cornbread
Pillowy Cornbread
Serves 4
Ingredients
  • ½ cup all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup yellow cornmeal, pulsed finer if medium-grind
  • ⅓ cup cane sugar
  • 1½ tsp baking powder
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 1 large egg
  • ½ cup whole milk
  • 2⅔ tbsp sour cream
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter, melted and slightly cooled
  • 1 tbsp canola oil
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract
Instructions
  1. Heat oven to 350°F with a rack in the middle. Grease a baking dish well — a 9×14-inch casserole dish suits 12 servings; use a smaller dish for fewer.
  2. If your cornmeal is medium-grind, pulse it in a blender until finer and sand-like.
  3. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, cornmeal, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
  4. In a separate bowl, whisk the eggs, milk, sour cream, melted butter, oil, and vanilla until smooth.
  5. Pour the wet into the dry and stir just until combined — don't overmix. If you used medium-grind cornmeal, let the batter sit 10 min.
  6. Pour into the dish, spread evenly, and bake 28–36 min. Start checking at 28. It's done when the center is set and a toothpick comes out with a few moist crumbs.
  7. Rest 10–15 min before cutting.
Tips
  • Don't overbake — that matters more than anything else for keeping it pillowy.
  • Brush the top with a little melted butter right out of the oven to keep the surface soft and add flavor.

Chicken Tortilla Soup
Chicken Tortilla Soup
Serves 4
Ingredients
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 jalapeño, diced (optional)
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp dried oregano
  • ½ tsp chili powder
  • 15 oz canned diced tomatoes (one 15-oz can)
  • 15 oz red enchilada sauce (one 15-oz can)
  • 3 cups chicken broth
  • 1 lb raw chicken breast
  • 15 oz canned black beans, drained and rinsed (one 15-oz can)
  • 1 cup corn
  • ½ cup sour cream
  • 1 lime, juiced
Instructions
  1. Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium. Add the onion and jalapeño and cook about 5 min, until softened.
  2. Add the garlic, cumin, smoked paprika, oregano, and chili powder. Cook 30 seconds, just until fragrant.
  3. Stir in the tomatoes, enchilada sauce, and broth. Add the raw chicken breasts, mostly submerged. Bring to a gentle simmer, lower the heat, partially cover, and cook 15–20 min, until the thickest part reaches 165°F.
  4. Remove the chicken and rest 5 min. Add the black beans and corn and simmer uncovered while you shred the chicken with two forks. Return it to the pot.
  5. Simmer uncovered another 5–10 min, depending on how thick you want it.
  6. Turn the heat to low. Stir a few spoonfuls of hot soup into the sour cream until smooth, then stir that back into the pot. Stir in the lime juice.
  7. Taste and add salt if needed. Keep warm over low heat without boiling. Serve with shredded cheese, avocado, sour cream, cilantro, lime wedges, and crushed tortilla chips.
Tips
  • Don't boil the chicken hard — a gentle simmer keeps chicken breast much more tender.
  • The uncovered simmer at the end is what makes this thick instead of watery.

Bell Pepper Tomato Sauce
Bell Pepper Tomato Sauce
Serves 4
Ingredients
  • 28 oz canned tomatoes, whole peeled or crushed
  • 1 bell pepper, diced small
  • ½ medium onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • ½ tsp dried basil
  • ½ tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • Pinch of red pepper flakes (optional)
  • ½ tsp sugar (optional)
  • 1 tbsp butter, for finishing (optional)
Instructions
  1. Heat the olive oil in a saucepan over medium. Add the onion, bell pepper, and a small pinch of salt. Cook 7–10 min, stirring occasionally, until very soft.
  2. Add the garlic, oregano, basil, and red pepper flakes. Cook 30–60 seconds, just until fragrant.
  3. Pour in the tomatoes. If using whole peeled, crush them with your hands first or break them up in the pot with a wooden spoon.
  4. Add the salt and black pepper. Bring to a gentle simmer.
  5. Reduce to medium-low and simmer 25–35 min, partially covered with a good gap for steam. Stir every 5–10 min.
  6. Blend to your preferred texture with an immersion blender — partially, so a little texture remains but the pepper and onion melt into the tomatoes.
  7. Taste and add salt if needed. If the tomatoes taste acidic, add up to ½ tsp sugar. Stir in the butter right at the end for a richer, silkier sauce.
Tips
  • Let the pepper and onion get really soft — that's the base of the flavor.
  • Taste before adding sugar. Good tomatoes often don't need it.

Cacio e Pepe
Cacio e Pepe
Serves 4
Ingredients
  • 1 lb spaghetti or tonnarelli
  • 2 tsp whole black peppercorns, coarsely cracked
  • 5 oz Pecorino Romano, finely grated
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter (optional)
  • 1½ cups pasta water, reserved
Instructions
  1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil and salt it lightly, since the Pecorino is salty. Cook the pasta until just shy of al dente, about 2 min short of the package time.
  2. While the pasta cooks, toast the cracked pepper in a large dry skillet over medium heat, swirling, until fragrant, about 1 min. Turn off the heat.
  3. Put the grated Pecorino in a large bowl. Ladle in about ½ cup of the hot pasta water a little at a time, stirring with a fork into a smooth, thick paste.
  4. Drain the pasta, reserving the rest of the cooking water. Add the pasta to the skillet with the pepper along with ½ cup pasta water and toss over low heat for 1 min so the starchy water coats every strand.
  5. Remove the skillet from the heat. Add the cheese paste and the butter, if using, and toss vigorously with tongs, splashing in more pasta water a tablespoon at a time, until the sauce turns glossy and clings to the pasta.
  6. Serve at once in warm bowls with more Pecorino and cracked pepper on top.
Tips
  • Everything depends on temperature. Boiling water melts Pecorino into strings, so make the paste with water that has cooled a moment and finish the pasta off the heat.
  • Grate the cheese yourself on a Microplane. Pre-grated Pecorino is coated with anti-caking starch and won't emulsify.
